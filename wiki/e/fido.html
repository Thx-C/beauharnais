Fido

Commandant en Second du 4e D'arty - Sidequest king

Biographie

Career wise on NW, Fido started as a ranker then a flag staff in 112. When 4e d'Arty opened, he decided to leave 112e to join it. This decision cost him his rank and position but it was definetly worth it.
Nowadays, Fido is the Executive Officer of 4e d'Arty and living his best life.

Outside of NW, this guy have so many hobbies and sidequest ongoing that I couldn't fill them here.
Among them you can find organ playing, tanks and even drinking !

He's also an artist/dev, he's often saw working on projects such as 4e Etat House or different GFXs.
